Horse names

One per line in program order. A leading 7. names that horse; a blank line leaves it alone. Lines 21–24 are the also-eligibles: name them ahead of time; they join the field only when they replace a scratched horse.

Scratches

A scratched horse is replaced by number, as at Churchill: pick the number that draws in (one of 21–24 not yet used) and the cup that was the scratched horse becomes that number on its own, tokens and all. Nothing moves on the mantle. Tick No replacement to scratch the horse outright: its cup shows the X and its tokens leave the pot.
