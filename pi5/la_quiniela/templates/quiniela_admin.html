Cups

The cup number is the one the horse command takes (the screen’s CUP n plus one). Pick a horse to assign it. A horse scratched with no replacement, one replaced, or an also-eligible not yet in the field is listed but not offered.

Horse names

One per line in program order. A leading 7. names that horse; a blank line leaves it alone. Lines 21–24 are the also-eligibles: name them ahead of time; they join the field only when they replace a scratched horse.

Scratches

A scratched horse is replaced by number, as at Churchill: pick the number that draws in (one of 21–24 not yet used) and its cup becomes that number, tokens and all. Nothing moves on the mantle. Tick No replacement to scratch the cup at the gateway instead; its tokens leave the pot.
